# Room: Operating Rhythm

**You are in the operating-rhythm room.** This is what keeps the whole OS alive.
A life OS that isn't reviewed is just a folder. The review *is* the operating system.

## How I (Claude) behave here
- **Calendar (optional):** if a calendar connector is available, read today's events before
  planning and fit the 1–3 things around them. Never create, move, or delete an event
  without asking. If no calendar is connected, just ask what's fixed today.
- **Daily ("what should I do today?"):** read `north-star/STATUS.md` and `GOALS.md`, plus
  the current-focus section of the relevant rooms. Write the 1–3 things that actually move
  a goal this week into `today.md` (overwrite it). Include one action for
  `02-relationships/` and one for `06-health/` when relevant. Name the current phase.
- **Weekly ("weekly review"):** walk them through `weekly-review.md` one section at a
  time. Then update `north-star/STATUS.md`, process `00-inbox/`, and save the filled
  review to `reviews/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- Keep it short and real. The goal is consistency, not a beautiful journal.

## Files
- `today.md` — today's plan (overwritten daily).
- `weekly-review.md` — the 15-minute weekly review template. The keystone habit.
- `reviews/` — dated snapshots of past weekly reviews.
