If the decision above is empty, ask what decision they want the board to weigh in on, then stop and wait.

## Steps
1. Read `01-identity/board-of-advisors.md` (roster, lenses, voices, blind spots) and
   `01-identity/profile.md` (their situation + the Shared briefing). If the board file still
   has `{{placeholders}}`, say the board isn't set up yet and offer `/board-setup` instead.
   Optionally read the relevant `north-star/` files or run `node brain/brain.js query "<topic>"`.
2. For **each advisor listed**, give their take **in their own voice** — the way they
   actually talk, the frameworks they'd reach for, applied to *this* decision and *this*
   person. Let them disagree with the user where their philosophy would.
3. **Where they agree / where they disagree** — short and plain. Real tension, not
   manufactured consensus.
4. **The synthesis — what to actually do.** Ground it in the profile's Shared briefing and
   constraints. Weigh each advisor's blind spot. Be decisive: one clear recommendation,
   not a menu.

## Rules
- Be the **filter, not the hype man.** If the decision matches a derailer from the profile,
  say so plainly.
- Keep each voice tight. Specifics over vibes.
- Trace the recommendation to a goal in `north-star/GOALS.md` where one applies.
- End with **ONE concrete next action** they can take today.
