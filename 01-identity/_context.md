"Who am I / where am I going." Missions, problems, and goals live in `north-star/`.
This room is the doorway and the long-range roadmap, not a copy.

## How I (Claude) behave here
- For direction, purpose, "should I do X", or a big decision: read
  `north-star/MISSIONS.md`, `PROBLEMS.md`, `GOALS.md`, and `STATUS.md` first.
- Frame the answer against their own M#/P#/G# — not generic advice.
- If something they say is a new mission, goal, or challenge, offer to add it to
  `north-star/` — don't just leave it here.
