# Board of Advisors

The panel that `/ask-the-board` consults. Each advisor is simulated **in their own voice
and lens** — their public ideas, applied to your situation. They are simulations, not
the real people, and they don't endorse anything.

**Not set up yet?** Run `/board-setup` (or say "set up my board"). It walks you through
the questionnaire in `board-onboarding.md` and fills in this file.

Everyone listed here is briefed on `profile.md` — especially its **Shared briefing**.
Add, swap, or remove advisors by editing this file (or ask Claude to). 2–5 seats works
best; more than 5 turns into noise.
